What the verdict means


verified
Returned identifiers and pinned inclusion agree at observation times. This is no independent trust or finality proof.
observed-null
A relevant response was null. Absence, failed execution and pruning are not established.
insufficient-evidence
An error, malformed response, cancellation or budget prevented verification.
inconsistent-observation
Observations disagree. Tip changes or backend lag may cause this; no endpoint misconduct claim.
